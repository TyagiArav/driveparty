DriveParty

Watch Drive videos together.

Paste a Google Drive video link, send your friend the invite, and play, pause and skip in sync, with chat on the side.

Start a party

The file must be shared as Anyone with the link. MP4 (H.264) and WebM play in all browsers.

Join a party

  1. 1. Share the fileIn Drive, set sharing to “Anyone with the link”.
  2. 2. Invite a friendSend them the party link or the 6-letter code.
  3. 3. Watch in syncAnyone can play, pause or seek, and everyone follows.
